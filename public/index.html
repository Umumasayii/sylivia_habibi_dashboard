How the numbers are calculated

  1. Eligibility. Only submissions aged 18–24, 25–34, 35–44 or 45–50, living in Rwanda and agreeing to participate are analysed.
  2. Answer codes. Strongly disagree = 1, Disagree = 2, Agree = 3, Strongly agree = 4. ‘Not sure’ is treated as missing.
  3. Reverse-scoring. For Q6, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q11, Q12, Q13, Q14, Q17, Q18, agreeing suggests less self-clarity, so score = 5 − code. After this, a higher score always means clearer self-understanding.
  4. Theme scores. Mean of a respondent's available item scores in each theme (1–4): Who I am Q6–Q10, Myself among others Q11–Q15, Strengths, criticism and mistakes Q16–Q20, Values and actions Q21–Q23, Emotions and reflection Q24–Q26. Overall = mean of Q6–Q26.
  5. Uncertainty. 95% confidence intervals use the t distribution and need at least 2 respondents. Cronbach's α and item–total correlations need at least 3 complete responses.
  6. Both form versions. Answers from the first version (statements S1–S21) are merged with the current one (Q6–Q26): Sn = Qn+5.
